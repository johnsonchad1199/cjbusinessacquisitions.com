Our Commitments

How we work with owners

How we work with every owner who reaches out, whether or not a transaction ever happens.

Confidentiality First

Every conversation and submission is treated as strictly confidential. We do not contact your employees, customers, or competitors without your permission.

Operator's Perspective

We evaluate your business the way an operator would — understanding your people, processes, and equipment, not just your financial statements.

Continuity for Your Team

We aim to preserve what already works: your employees, your customer relationships, and the reputation you spent years building.

Financing

How we finance acquisitions

We acquire businesses using a combination of an equity injection, SBA financing, and seller financing.

Equity Injection

Buyer capital invested directly into the transaction at close.

SBA Financing

SBA-backed acquisition financing through an established lender.

Seller Financing

A seller note that keeps both sides aligned through the transition.

Proof of funds with every LOI

Once we have reviewed the business and financials, we are happy to provide proof of funds with any LOI we submit to demonstrate our ability to finance the transaction.
