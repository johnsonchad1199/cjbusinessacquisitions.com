For Business Owners & Brokers

What You Can Expect Working With Us

Selling a business you've built is personal. Our process is designed to be confidential, respectful of your time, and focused on continuity for your people and customers.

Our Commitments

How We Work With Owners

Confidentiality First

Every conversation and submission is treated as strictly confidential. We do not contact employees, customers, or competitors without your permission.

Operator's Perspective

We evaluate your business the way an operator would — understanding your people, processes, and equipment, not just the financial statements.

Continuity for Your Team

We aim to preserve what already works: your employees, your customer relationships, and the reputation you've built.

The Process

What Happens After You Reach Out

  1. Confidential Introduction

    You submit a brief overview of your business through our confidential form, or reach out directly. No information is shared outside our team.

  2. Initial Conversation

    We schedule a call to learn more about your business, your goals, and your timeline — and answer any questions you have about us.

  3. Mutual NDA

    If there's a fit, we execute a non-disclosure agreement before exchanging any detailed financial or operational information.

  4. Evaluation & Diligence

    We take the time to understand your operations, financials, customers, and team — always working around your schedule and discretion needs.

  5. Structuring the Transaction

    We work with you to structure a transaction and transition timeline — typically 6 to 12 months — that works for you, your employees, and your customers.

  6. Closing & Transition

    We support a smooth handoff, with continuity for your team and customers as the top priority.

Brokers & Intermediaries

Working With Brokers

We welcome hearing from business brokers, M&A advisors, and other intermediaries representing sellers in the Piedmont Triad's engineering and manufacturing sectors. We move efficiently, communicate directly, and respect the confidentiality your clients expect.
